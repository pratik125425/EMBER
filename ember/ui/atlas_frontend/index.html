      Earth-normal air or a lunar habitat atmosphere?

      Lower cabin pressure makes spacewalk prep faster and structures lighter, so NASA's Exploration Atmosphere raises the oxygen share instead. Scrub between the two atmospheres. Gravity stays fixed; only oxygen and pressure change.

      Switch gravity and watch the evidence go dark.

      NASA's fire tests were run in orbit and on the ground. Bases on the Moon and Mars will have some gravity, and the official PSI archive holds no fire test there. Pick a regime: tested evidence lights up, and gaps stay visible.
